## Stack

User-approved React and TypeScript interface, one-command local EVM demo, optional public testnet deployment. Vite, viem and Solidity implement that approach.

## Users

ETHOnline judges and developers evaluating a reproducible, testnet-only prediction-market position. Inferred secondary audience: liquidity providers exploring the curve.

## Product Purpose

Show Gaussian prediction-market pricing executing through official 1inch Aqua and a custom SwapVM instruction, including observable token transfers. The sole target is the $5,000 Build an Aqua App track. Krishna Agarwal is the solo builder.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Test assets only. Free local execution must be complete. Public deployments are reported only after verification. Manual resolution must be visible. Numerical results and supported domains must be documented honestly.

## Brand Commitments

GaussVM. User-requested neumorphic interface. Clear language, accessible contrast, no invented statistics or guarantees.

## Evidence on Hand

Pinned upstream contract source and verified Paradigm equations. No public deployment or runtime evidence at initialization.

## Product Principles

Make the mechanism visible. Use receipts for transaction success. Separate an illustrative curve from executable quotes. Keep liquidity custody and resolution authority understandable.
